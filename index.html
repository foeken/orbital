⌘K runs everything

Press ⌘K anywhere and type a few letters of what you want: “pin” finds Pin to today, “mti” finds Move to Inbox. ↑ ↓ choose, ↩ runs. The list follows your caret, so on a task it offers that task’s status, assignee and pins. Not sure what something is called? Open ⌘K and browse.

Plan your day

Home is the Work View: the Timeline, with what changed and what came in, beside My Tasks. New tasks land in your Inbox; tick one to accept it and tick again when it is done. ⌃⇧D opens Today, and ⌘⇧Space adds a task from any app.

Make the keys yours

Highlight any row in ⌘K and press ⇧⌘K to give it your own shortcut. ⌘N opens another window, ⌥⌘N splits this one in two and ⌘\ jumps between the halves. Set as Home in ⌘K chooses where the house button takes you.
