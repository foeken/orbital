    Typed at the start of a row, - or * starts a bullet, 1. a numbered item, # to ### a heading, > a quote, [] a checkbox, three backticks a code block and --- a divider. Typed inline, **bold**, *italic*, ~~strike~~ and `code` turn into marks as you close them.

    Fixed keys and your own

    Every key on this page except the few below is a palette row with a default, and you can change it: highlight the row in ⌘K and press ⇧⌘K. Your keys follow you to every Mac you sign in on. The keys below are fixed, and the recorder refuses them with the reason.

    ActionKeysSee
    The command palette⌘K⌘K
    Record a key⇧⌘K⌘K
    Larger text, smaller text, reset⇧⌘+ ⇧⌘- ⌘0Settings
    Delete the block⇧⌘⌫Writing
    Move the row or selection⇧⌘↑ ⇧⌘↓Writing

    A recorded key needs ⌘ or ⌃, or is a function key on its own such as F6. Sync, Focus graph, Show graph and most other rows start without a key, waiting for one of yours.
