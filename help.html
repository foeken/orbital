⌘K runs everything

Press ⌘K anywhere and type a few letters of what you want: “pin” finds Pin to today, “sti” finds Set status to Inbox. ↑ ↓ choose, ↩ runs. The list follows your caret, so on a task it offers that task’s status, assignee and pins. Not sure what something is called? Open ⌘K and browse.

Plan your day

Home is the Work View: the Timeline, with what changed and what came in, beside My Tasks. New tasks land in your Inbox; tick one to accept it and tick again when it is done. ⇧⌘Space creates a task: type its title, pick a type with ↑ ↓ and someone with ⇥ if you like, and press ↩. ⌃⇧D opens Today.

Make the keys yours

Highlight any row in ⌘K and press ⇧⌘K to give it your own shortcut. ⌘N opens a pane beside this one, ⌥⌘N another window and ⌘\ jumps between the panes. Set as Home in ⌘K chooses where the house button takes you.
